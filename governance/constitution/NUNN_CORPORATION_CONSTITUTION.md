# Preamble

The Nunn Corporation Constitution is the highest governing document of the Corporation.

Its purpose is to establish the enduring principles, governance philosophy, authority structure, and organizational responsibilities that guide every business unit, subsidiary, executive, employee, contractor, technology platform, and governance document operating under the authority of Nunn Corporation.

The Constitution is intended to remain stable over time. Operational methods, technologies, policies, standards, and procedures may evolve, but all such documents derive their authority from the constitutional principles established herein.

Governance within Nunn Corporation exists to promote responsible leadership, transparency, accountability, stewardship, ethical conduct, sustainable growth, and long-term organizational success.

---

# Article I
# Constitutional Authority

## Section 1.01 Purpose

This Constitution establishes the governing authority of Nunn Corporation.

It defines the principles under which the Corporation shall operate and establishes the hierarchy from which all subordinate governance documents derive their authority.

---

## Section 1.02 Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution is the supreme governing authority of Nunn Corporation.

All subordinate governance documents shall remain consistent with the principles established herein.

In the event of conflict between governing documents, the document possessing the higher constitutional authority shall prevail.

---

## Section 1.03 Hierarchy of Authority

Nunn Corporation shall maintain the following governance hierarchy.

1. Constitution
2. Corporate Charter
3. Executive Governance
4. Board Governance
5. Management Operating Book (MOB)
6. Enterprise Manuals
7. Corporate Policies
8. Standards
9. Procedures
10. Operational Registers

Each level derives its authority from the level immediately preceding it.

No subordinate document may conflict with a superior governing document.

---

## Section 1.04 Interpretation

Questions regarding governance shall be resolved by referencing higher governing authority until constitutional intent is established.

Interpretation shall favor:

- Organizational Integrity
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Security
- Stewardship
- Human Responsibility
- Long-Term Sustainability

---

## Section 1.05 Amendment Authority

Only the governing authority responsible for this Constitution may amend its provisions.

No subordinate document may alter, reinterpret, weaken, or supersede constitutional authority through implication or omission.

# Article II
# Foundational Governance Rules

The following rules govern every subordinate document, policy, platform, system, committee, department, executive, employee, and technology operating under Nunn Corporation.

---

## Governance Rule No. 1
### Hierarchical Consistency

No lower-level governance document may conflict with, weaken, reinterpret, or supersede a higher-level governing document.

---

## Governance Rule No. 2
### Human Accountability

Artificial intelligence may assist decision-making but shall not assume responsibility for decisions reserved to accountable human authority.

Human leadership remains responsible for executive, legal, financial, ethical, governance, and strategic decisions.

---

## Governance Rule No. 3
### Verifiable Action

Material decisions, approvals, and actions shall be attributable, traceable, and auditable through appropriate records.

---

## Governance Rule No. 4
### Least Authority

Authority, privileges, permissions, and access shall be limited to the minimum necessary to accomplish authorized responsibilities.

---

## Governance Rule No. 5
### Evidence Before Assertion

Strategic, financial, operational, compliance, legal, and governance decisions shall be supported by verifiable evidence whenever reasonably practicable.

---

## Governance Rule No. 6
### Security by Design

Security, privacy, resilience, and auditability shall be incorporated into organizational systems, technologies, and governance practices from inception.

---

## Governance Rule No. 7
### Continuous Stewardship

Governance is an ongoing executive responsibility.

Policies, standards, procedures, technologies, and organizational practices shall be reviewed and improved through disciplined oversight.

---

# Constitutional Statement

The Constitution establishes enduring principles.

The Charter establishes organizational authority.

Executive Governance establishes strategic direction.

The Board provides oversight.

The Management Operating Book governs execution.

Policies establish organizational expectations.

Standards establish consistency.

Procedures establish repeatable operations.

Operational Registers provide evidence of execution.

Each document supports, but never supersedes, the authority of those above it.

---

# Ratification

This Constitution shall become effective upon formal approval by the authorized governing authority of Nunn Corporation.

All future governance documents shall acknowledge and derive authority from this Constitution.
