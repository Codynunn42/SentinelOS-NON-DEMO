## 1) Executive Summary

Nunn Corporation's strategy for 2026-2030 is to define and lead a new category: **Executive AI Governance**.

The market is transitioning from model-centric competition ("who has the best model") to governance-centric adoption ("who can govern AI responsibly, prove outcomes, and preserve human authority"). Nunn Corporation will lead this transition by building and scaling Sentinel as the **Executive Operating System** for governed AI, infrastructure accountability, and evidence-based decision making.

### Core Strategic Position

Nunn Corporation builds Executive Operating Systems that govern AI, infrastructure, and critical workflows with evidence-based decision making.

### Strategic Outcome by 2030

By 2030, Nunn Corporation will be recognized as the reference architecture and operating standard for trusted AI governance across federal, critical infrastructure, and enterprise environments.

### Board-Level Priorities

1. Institutionalize executive doctrine: Govern AI. Enable Decisions. Preserve Human Authority.
2. Productize governance as software: Sentinel Executive OS as the control plane.
3. Standardize proof-before-scale gates across all engagements.
4. Operationalize model independence and policy portability.
5. Lead federal and infrastructure programs with accountable orchestration.
6. Publish measurable outcomes tied to governance, economics, and community stewardship.

- Policy-bound workflows
- Evidence retention and auditability

2. Evidence-backed execution and executive decision traceability.
3. Model-agnostic orchestration with policy and security continuity.
4. Infrastructure and community accountability integrated into delivery.
5. Repeatable, auditable readiness for regulated and federal environments.

## 13) KPI Scorecard and Quarterly Gate Thresholds

### KPI Domains and Definitions

1. Governance Integrity

- Percent of critical workflows with explicit approval chains
- Policy conformance rate
- Decision register completeness rate

1. Evidence Assurance

- Receipt generation coverage for governed actions
- Audit retrieval success rate
- Evidence retention SLA attainment

1. Operational Performance

- Workflow cycle time improvement
- Exception resolution time
- Pilot-to-scale conversion rate

1. AI Control and Independence

- Multi-model portability coverage
- Guardrail violation rate
- Evaluation pass rate by use case

1. Economic Outcomes

- Cost avoidance realized
- ROI attainment versus plan
- Time-to-value against target milestones

1. Risk and Resilience

- Critical risk closure velocity
- DR exercise pass rate
- Dependency concentration reduction

1. Community and Infrastructure Stewardship

- Accountability review completion rate
- Stakeholder communication timeliness
- Workforce enablement participation

### Quarterly Gate Thresholds (Minimum)

1. Gate A: Governance Baseline
>
- >= 90% critical workflows with named authority chain
- >= 95% policy mapping coverage for in-scope workflows

1. Gate B: Evidence Baseline
>
- >= 95% receipt coverage on governed production actions
- >= 99% evidence retrieval success for sampled audits

1. Gate C: Pilot Effectiveness
>
- >= 20% cycle-time improvement against pre-pilot baseline
- <= 5% unresolved high-severity workflow exceptions

1. Gate D: AI Governance Health
>
- >= 2 provider-ready model pathways for mission-critical flows
- <= 1% guardrail breach rate on governed execution paths

1. Gate E: Economics and Scale Readiness
>
- >= 1.5x validated benefit-to-cost signal at pilot stage
- Documented scale economics signed by executive sponsor

1. Gate F: Risk and Accountability

- 100% high/critical risks with named owner and mitigation plan
- 100% infrastructure accountability review completion before scale

### Board Escalation Triggers

1. Any high-severity governance breach unresolved beyond 10 business days.
2. Any evidence audit retrieval failure above 1% in monthly sample.
3. Any mission-critical model concentration above 80% without contingency path.
4. Any scale recommendation lacking executive decision artifacts.
