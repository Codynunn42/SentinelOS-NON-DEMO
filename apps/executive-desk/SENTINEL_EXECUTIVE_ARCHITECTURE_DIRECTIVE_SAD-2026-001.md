# Sentinel Executive Architecture Directive

**Directive ID:** SAD-2026-001  
**Title:** Sentinel Absorption Principle and Executive Orchestration  
**Status:** Approved  
**Decision Authority:** Executive Leadership  
**Effective:** Immediately

## Purpose

Establish Sentinel AI as the single executive orchestration authority for the Sentinel platform and define the long-term architectural direction for capability evolution.

## Executive Decision

Sentinel AI shall evolve through **capability absorption**, not through the accumulation of independent orchestration systems.

Existing and future orchestration concepts shall become native Sentinel capabilities whenever practical and aligned with governance, security, and executive operations.

## Architectural Principle

> **Sentinel grows by absorbing capabilities, not by accumulating separate systems.**
> Every significant capability should strengthen the unified Sentinel platform rather than introduce additional executive control planes.

## Executive Architecture

### Sentinel AI

The executive intelligence platform responsible for:

- Executive reasoning
- Decision support
- Planning
- Governance
- Executive orchestration

### Executive Cortex

The native Sentinel orchestration layer responsible for:

- Intent classification
- Task planning
- Capability routing
- Context assembly
- Multi-agent coordination
- Approval workflows
- Executive synthesis

### SentinelOS

The execution platform responsible for:

- Executing approved operations
- Providing governed services
- Returning structured results
- Producing operational evidence

### Clarity Memory

The organizational memory responsible for:

- Persistent executive context
- Decision history
- Evidence relationships
- Organizational knowledge
- Context continuity

### Executive Trust

The governance layer responsible for:

- Policy enforcement
- Evidence validation
- Confidence assessment
- Audit receipts
- Human approval controls

## Tilda Absorption Decision

The orchestration concepts developed within the internal Tilda project will be **absorbed into Sentinel AI** as part of the Executive Cortex.

Tilda will not continue as an independent orchestration platform.

Its architectural strengths will become native Sentinel capabilities.

## Milestone Reference

**Milestone:** Executive Cortex Foundation

### Objective

Establish Sentinel AI as the single orchestration authority while preserving SentinelOS as the governed execution platform.

### Success Criteria

- Sentinel AI functions as the sole executive orchestration layer.
- Executive Cortex architecture is formally defined.
- SentinelOS executes governed capabilities under Sentinel direction.
- Clarity Memory provides persistent organizational context.
- Executive Trust governs all recommendations and state-changing operations.
- No competing orchestration layers exist within the Sentinel architecture.

## Implementation Priority

**Next Priority (Phase 2)**

## Executive North Star

> **One Executive Brain. One Operating System. One Governance Model.**
> Sentinel AI serves as the executive intelligence platform.
>
> Executive Cortex plans, routes, and governs.
>
> SentinelOS executes.
>
> Clarity Memory remembers.
>
> Executive Trust verifies.
>

## Closing Statement

This directive establishes the long-term architectural philosophy for Sentinel AI. Future enhancements shall be evaluated according to their ability to strengthen the unified Sentinel platform, simplify executive operations, and preserve governance, evidence, and trust.
