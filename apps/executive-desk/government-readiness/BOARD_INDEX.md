## Executive Summary

GBP Engineering Foundation is complete for the current implemented scope. The platform now has governed command surfaces, evidence generation, drift validation, recovery verification, bridge-gap analysis, and certification reporting.

This board index connects the engineering foundation to the operating doctrine that will govern the platform in the next phase: mission package delivery, operational readiness advancement, and evidence-backed certification posture.

## Board Decision Context

The strategic shift is from feature configuration to operational doctrine.

The board should evaluate the platform as a governed institutional capability with three priorities:

1. Maintain strict traceability from North Star to certification evidence.
2. Advance mission packages only through explicit ORL and promotion policy controls.
3. Preserve read-only doctrine for diagnostic and planning modes until authority-approved execution is required.

## Governance Chain

North Star -> Doctrine -> Bridge Gap Report -> Remediation Plan -> Implementation -> Gate Validation -> Evidence -> Certification

This chain is mandatory for every board-visible claim.

## Board-Level Operating Model

### Daily

- Review bridge-gap posture.
- Confirm receipts and audit continuity.
- Confirm open blockers and evidence deltas.

### Weekly

- Review the ORL scorecard.
- Validate blocker trends and readiness movement.
- Re-sequence light-mode remediation steps.

### Monthly

- Review certification posture.
- Confirm whether a mission package can advance.
- Update executive and board summaries.
