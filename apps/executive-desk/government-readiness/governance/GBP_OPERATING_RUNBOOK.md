# GBP Operating Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook points to the one-page GBP chief-of-staff brief and keeps the operational reference available for execution.

## Primary Brief

Use [GBP Chief of Staff Brief](GBP_CHIEF_OF_STAFF_BRIEF.md) as the main operating summary.

## Operating Rule

Use outcome-first language. Focus on relationship building, readiness evidence, and the next government engagement step.

## GBP Expectations

This package is not generic government content. It must reflect the way they want it: direct, outcome-first, evidence-backed, and ready for relationship building.

## What Is Ready

- Executive Desk governance, cadence, and board reporting are established.
- Government messaging, outreach binder, and outcome-first positioning are established.
- Sentinel AI scan/status and efficiency planning are wired into Executive Desk.
- Local read-only verification is complete for the Executive Desk loop.

## What To Focus On

- GBP mission package references and the operating runbook.
- Government relationship building and follow-up cadence.
- The way they want it: expectations, directional guidance, and government-specific execution style.
- Pilot readiness, discovery flow, and one-pager polish.
- Sentinel AI runtime verification and evidence-backed scan output.
- Revenue motion that converts outreach into meetings, pilots, and advisory work.

## Daily Operating Sequence

1. Review the GBP mission package, operating summary, and current government contact list.
2. Ask Sentinel AI to scan using the current logic:
   - What is ready to go?
   - What should we focus on now?
   - Where are the biggest government-readiness gaps?
3. Capture the top three focus areas and the top three ready-to-go items.
4. Update the contact list, follow-ups, and pilot materials.
5. Record evidence for any claim or readiness statement.

## Weekly Operating Sequence

- Refresh the government binder and executive collateral.
- Review relationship progress and next-step commitments.
- Reconcile GBP references in the operating runbook with the latest mission package.
- Confirm Sentinel AI scan output still matches the current operating logic.
- Review pilot readiness and launch blockers.

## Sentinel AI Scan Prompt

Use this logic when asking Sentinel AI to scan:

- Show what is ready to go.
- Show what should be focused on next.
- Surface government relationship-building gaps.
- Surface readiness gaps in the GBP mission package.
- Surface evidence gaps before public claims or outreach.
- Keep the result concise and action-oriented.

## Evidence To Capture

- Government outreach artifacts used.
- Meeting notes and follow-up commitments.
- Updated GBP references in the runbook.
- Sentinel AI scan output.
- Any readiness blockers or open questions.
