# Controlled Operations Cadence Directive

Company: Nunn Corporation | Nunn Cloud LLC  
Program: Nunn Corporation 2030  
Date Effective: 2026-07-18  
Issued By: Office of the CEO

## Directive Purpose

Establish the formal transition from high-velocity validation mode to controlled operations mode, with predictability as the execution standard for all internal teams and all government-facing engagements.

## Executive Position

We are aligned on operating posture.

1. Controlled operations is now the default mode.
2. Expectations will be met exactly as set.
3. Scale decisions remain evidence-conditioned.
4. Government partner confidence will be sustained through consistent cadence execution.

## Operating Standard

Predictability is the standard in everything we do.

For every milestone, workflow, and release decision:

1. Gate requirements must be clear.
2. Named authority ownership must be explicit.
3. Evidence artifacts must be complete and retrievable.
4. Decision outcomes must be recorded in the decision register.
5. Exceptions must be escalated within defined SLA windows.

## Cadence Structure (Effective Immediately)

### Daily Cadence

1. Executive Intelligence Brief publication.
2. Gate delta review (new passes, regressions, pending controls).
3. Evidence completeness check (receipt and audit artifact status).
4. Risk and dependency change log update.
5. Day-end action closure log.

Daily output artifacts:

- Daily intelligence brief
- Daily gate delta summary
- Daily risk and dependency log

### Weekly Cadence

1. Program Gate Council.
2. KPI trend review against targets.
3. Federal readiness posture review.
4. Corrective action assignment and due dates.
5. Executive decision review for pending approvals.

Weekly output artifacts:

- Weekly gate council notes
- Weekly KPI variance report
- Weekly federal readiness status

### Monthly Cadence

1. Executive Strategy Review.
2. Evidence and decision-register audit.
3. Infrastructure accountability review.
4. Economic proof gate review.
5. Go/hold recommendations for next milestone set.

Monthly output artifacts:

- Monthly strategy scorecard
- Monthly evidence audit summary
- Monthly accountability and economics memo

### Quarterly Cadence

1. Board governance package review.
2. Gate threshold attestations.
3. Portfolio scale/no-scale decisions.
4. Federal partner assurance update.

Quarterly output artifacts:

- Board governance packet
- Quarterly gate attestation report
- Quarterly partner assurance summary

## Expectation Discipline and Escalation

1. Any unresolved high-severity governance issue older than 10 business days escalates to COO and CEO.
2. Any evidence retrieval failure above threshold escalates to Governance Lead and CTO.
3. Any variance against board-approved cadence outputs triggers corrective plan within 48 hours.
4. Any external claim must match current evidence posture and gate scope.

## Government Partner Assurance Statement

Nunn Corporation commits to controlled, policy-bound, and evidence-backed execution across all government engagements.

Predictability is not optional in our delivery model. It is a standing requirement.

This directive is considered operational when:

1. Daily, weekly, monthly cadence outputs are published on schedule.
2. Workbook ownership fields are populated with named assignees.
3. Escalation rules are observed with documented timestamps.
4. Partner-facing materials reflect predictability as the execution standard.
