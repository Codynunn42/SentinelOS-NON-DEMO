## Federal-Facing Mission Package Criteria

A federal-facing mission package must show:

- A clear capability statement
- A deterministic operating doctrine
- A bridge-gaps diagnosis and remediation path
- An ORL posture and advancement path
- A traceable evidence chain
- A defined authority and escalation model
