# Executive Review Checklist

## Review Scope

- Executive Board
- Master Operating Blueprint (MOB)
- Initiative Registry

## Review Objective

Ensure priorities remain aligned, unnecessary expansion is avoided, and government-first posture remains intact.

## Checklist

### Executive Board

- [ ] Current board priorities still map to government readiness goals.
- [ ] Decision cadence supports federal outreach and pilot readiness.
- [ ] Outcome accountability owners are explicit.

### Master Operating Blueprint (MOB)

- [ ] Government mission outcomes are represented as first-class priorities.
- [ ] Governance and authority controls are clearly integrated.
- [ ] Human-in-the-loop checkpoints are preserved.
- [ ] Evidence and reporting pathways are complete.
- [ ] GBP expectations and directional implementation guidelines reflect the way they want it.

### Initiative Registry

- [ ] Active initiatives support government-first posture.
- [ ] No initiative expands scope without outcome justification.
- [ ] Dependencies and sequencing are realistic.
- [ ] Near-term execution supports DOE engagement readiness.

## Risk Signals

- Priority drift away from government outcomes.
- Tool-first messaging replacing outcome-first messaging.
- Unowned actions in high-priority initiatives.
- Expansion without governance or evidence plan.
