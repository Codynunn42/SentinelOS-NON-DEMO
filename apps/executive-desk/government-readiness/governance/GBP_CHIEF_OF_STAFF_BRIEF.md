# GBP Chief of Staff Brief

## Purpose

Keep GBP execution focused on the way they want it: direct, outcome-first, evidence-backed, and built for relationship building.

## Executive Summary

Executive Desk is ready to support GBP execution with governed cadence, board reporting, MOB integration, and Sentinel AI scan logic that shows what is ready, what needs focus, and what evidence is missing before outreach or claims, while keeping craftsmanship, directness, and relationship-building front and center.

## GBP Expectations

- Start with the government outcome, not the product feature.
- Keep the tone concise, direct, and actionable.
- Tie every claim to evidence or a verified operating state.
- Avoid generic modernization language unless it advances the government outcome.
- Optimize for relationship building, readiness, and next-step clarity.

## Directional Implementation Rules

1. Lead with the mission outcome.
2. Show ready-to-go items first.
3. Show focus areas second.
4. Show blockers and evidence gaps last.
5. Keep the content short enough to use in a live operating review.

## Sentinel AI Scan Logic

Ask Sentinel AI to use my logic and craftsmanship standard to return what is ready to go, what to focus on now, what government-readiness gaps still matter, what evidence is missing before outreach or claims, and what GBP items should be polished first.

## Top 5 Ready

1. Executive Desk governance doctrine, cadence, and board reporting are established.
2. MOB integration and closeout-state workflow are operating.
3. Government messaging and outcome-first positioning artifacts are in place.
4. Local Gate 6-8 verification is complete for the read-only Executive Desk loop.
5. Sentinel AI scan/status routing is wired and ready to support readiness reviews.

## Top 5 Focus

1. GBP mission package references and implementation direction are kept explicit in active artifacts.
2. Government relationship building cadence and follow-up discipline are executed weekly.
3. Pilot readiness materials (one-pager, discovery flow, demo narrative) are polished to craftsmanship quality.
4. Sentinel AI runtime verification and evidence-backed output are confirmed before external claims.
5. Evidence gaps are closed before outreach, pilot commitments, or modernization claims.

## Daily Operating Loop

1. Review the GBP mission package and current contact list.
2. Run the Sentinel AI scan using the logic above.
3. Capture and update the Top 5 Ready and Top 5 Focus lists.
4. Update follow-ups, pilot materials, and GBP references.
5. Record evidence for any readiness claim.

## Weekly Operating Loop

- Refresh the government binder and executive collateral.
- Reconcile GBP references with the latest mission package.
- Confirm Sentinel AI scan output still matches current logic.
- Review relationship progress, pilot readiness, and launch blockers.

## Evidence To Keep

- Government outreach artifacts.
- Meeting notes and follow-up commitments.
- Updated GBP references.
- Sentinel AI scan output.
- Open readiness blockers.
