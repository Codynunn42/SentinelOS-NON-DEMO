# GBP Phase 6: Mission Activation Handoff Procedure

Date: 2026-07-18
Status: Draft for executive review
Scope: SentinelOS Executive Desk mission package activation and handoff

Mission first. Technology second.

## Purpose

Define the handoff procedure for activating a mission package after governance review and readiness validation.

This procedure is read-only until explicit activation authority is granted.

## Handoff Preconditions

Mission activation may proceed only when:

- The ORL scorecard is complete.
- The bridge-gaps diagnosis is attached.
- The light-mode remediation plan is attached.
- Authority mapping is verified.
- Required gate evidence is present.
- The certification promotion policy permits advancement.

## Handoff Participants

- Mission package owner
- Governance reviewer
- Platform authority owner
- Operator lead
- Executive approver (if required)

## Handoff Sequence

1. Confirm package identity and ORL posture.
2. Review the latest bridge-gaps report.
3. Review the scorecard and advancement checklist.
4. Confirm authority and escalation chain.
5. Confirm evidence attachment completeness.
6. Approve activation, hold, or rollback posture.
7. Record the decision in the cadence evidence log.

## Activation Modes

### Read-Only Activation Review

- Used for diagnosis and readiness review.
- No runtime change is performed.
- No deployment is initiated.

### Controlled Activation

- Used when authority approves the activation.
- Must be tied to a specific mission package.
- Must include rollback expectations and audit receipt.

### Deferred Activation

- Used when blockers remain unresolved.
- The package remains in review.
- Remediation must be completed before retry.

## Required Handoff Artifacts

- Mission package template
- ORL scorecard
- Bridge gaps report
- Light-mode remediation plan
- Evidence register
- Promotion decision record

## Handoff Checklist

- [ ] Package name confirmed
- [ ] ORL posture confirmed
- [ ] Authority chain confirmed
- [ ] Evidence complete
- [ ] Blockers reviewed
- [ ] Activation decision recorded
- [ ] Receipt issued

## Post-Handoff Responsibilities

After activation, the package owner must:

- Maintain cadence evidence.
- Continue bridge-gap monitoring.
- Report any new blockers immediately.
- Preserve read-only governance discipline for diagnosis modes.
