# Discovery Meeting Framework

## Objective

Confirm whether Executive Desk can improve decision readiness, governance alignment, and outcome execution for the target government mission context.

## Meeting Goals

- Understand mission objectives and near-term decision pressure.
- Surface governance, authority, and execution constraints.
- Identify measurable outcomes and risk considerations.
- Determine fit for a focused pilot or deeper assessment.

## Suggested Agenda (45-60 Minutes)

1. Context and mission objective (10 min)
2. Current operating model and blockers (10 min)
3. Decision and governance landscape (10 min)
4. Outcome and evidence requirements (10 min)
5. Alignment summary and next steps (5-10 min)

## Core Discovery Questions

- What mission outcome is most at risk if execution does not improve in the next 90 days?
- Which executive decisions are slowed by uncertainty or fragmented information?
- Where are authority or delegation boundaries unclear during execution?
- What evidence is required for oversight, audit, or leadership review?
- Which modernization efforts are active, and where are they encountering resistance?
- What would a successful first outcome look like in measurable terms?

## Qualification Signals

Strong fit:

- clear outcome pressure,
- active modernization constraints,
- need for cross-team coordination,
- demand for evidence and accountability.

Weak fit:

- no defined mission outcomes,
- no executive sponsor,
- no appetite for governed change process.
