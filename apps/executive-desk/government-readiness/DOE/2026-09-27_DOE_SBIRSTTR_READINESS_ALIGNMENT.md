## Executive Readout

Sentinel has a credible governance and evidence foundation for a DOE-facing
positioning narrative. The repository does not yet prove application readiness.
The largest gaps are registration confirmation, topic-specific Phase I design,
measurable feasibility gates, budget and team packaging, application-specific
IP and disclosure review, and support evidence.

The strongest verified DOE relationship signal is an OSBP response that offered
an introductory call and invited a capability sheet. That is a relationship
and routing opportunity, not DOE sponsorship, product validation, an award, or
a confirmed solicitation.

   publication, confidentiality, and subcontract review.

   disclosures required by the application.

**Proposed owners:** CFO/Commercial Lead, legal counsel, and Governance Lead.<br>
