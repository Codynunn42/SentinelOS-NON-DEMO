## 1) Executive Summary

Nunn Corporation's federal strategy is to serve as a **governance and orchestration partner** for AI-enabled and infrastructure-linked programs where accountability, human authority, and measurable outcomes are mandatory.

Our approach is not to compete as a model vendor. Our role is to provide the Executive Operating System that ensures AI and operational workflows are policy-bound, auditable, secure, and outcome-driven.

### Federal Positioning Statement

Nunn Corporation enables trusted execution for federal and infrastructure missions through governed workflow orchestration, human approval chains, evidence-grade audit trails, and milestone-based decision control.

Procurement-ready one-liner:
Nunn Corporation delivers policy-bound, evidence-backed execution systems that reduce mission risk while accelerating accountable modernization.

Predictability commitment for government partners:
Nunn Corporation adopts operational predictability as a standing federal standard. Every milestone, workflow overlay, and scale decision is executed in lockstep with documented gates, named authorities, and auditable evidence requirements.

## 11) Governance KPI Framework for Federal Programs

### Priority KPI Domains

1. Governance integrity (authority chain coverage, policy conformance)
2. Evidence assurance (receipt coverage, retrieval success)
3. Operational outcomes (cycle-time gains, exception closure)
4. AI control (guardrail adherence, multi-model readiness)
5. Economics (benefit-to-cost signal, time-to-value)
6. Risk and resilience (high-risk closure velocity, continuity performance)
7. Community stewardship (accountability completion and stakeholder transparency)

### Recommended Gate Thresholds
>
1. >= 90% authority-chain coverage in critical workflows
2. >= 95% receipt coverage on governed actions
3. >= 99% evidence retrieval success in sampled audits
4. >= 2 provider-ready pathways for mission-critical model flows
5. 100% high/critical risks with owner and mitigation plan
