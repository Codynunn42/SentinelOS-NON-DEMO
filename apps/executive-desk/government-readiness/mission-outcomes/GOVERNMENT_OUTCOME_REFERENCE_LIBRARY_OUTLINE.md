# Government Outcome Reference Library Outline

Organize references by outcomes, not products.

## Outcome Domains

- Executive Decision Support
- Workflow Modernization
- Evidence Collection
- Governance
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Executive Reporting
- Mission Assurance
- Risk Management
- Operational Visibility
- Cross-Department Coordination

## Domain Record Template

- Outcome domain:
- Mission problem addressed:

## Build Sequence

1. Define one canonical use case per domain.
2. Attach measurable baseline and target outcomes.
3. Add evidence references and reporting cadence.
4. Mark reuse value across agencies or departments.

## Guidance

Agencies evaluate solutions through mission results and accountability constraints. This structure mirrors that reality and strengthens executive credibility.
