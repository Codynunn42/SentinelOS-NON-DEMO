# Executive Intelligence Brief

This is the permanent standard format for Executive Desk intelligence output.

## Document Standard

- Audience: executive decision-makers and program leaders.
- Tone: concise, non-technical, outcome-focused.
- Cadence: daily or event-driven.
- Length target: 2-4 pages.
- Rule: every section should support a decision, risk view, or mission action.

## Required Sections

### Executive Summary

State the most important changes, why they matter now, and what decision pressure they create.

### Politics and Federal Policy

Summarize policy developments, administration signals, congressional activity, and appropriations or oversight context relevant to mission execution.

### DOE Intelligence

Capture DOE-specific developments, initiatives, leadership signals, and operational implications.

### AI and Technology

Highlight technology trends that impact mission execution, modernization pace, governance posture, or risk.

### Business and Markets

Summarize market dynamics, vendor movement, and capability shifts that affect timing, cost, or strategic options.

### Government Opportunities

Identify near-term and mid-term opportunities where Executive Desk can support decision quality, modernization, and measurable outcomes.

### Strategic Implications

Translate intelligence into implications for timing, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, and resource posture.

### Recommended Actions

Provide 3-5 concrete actions with owner, timeline, and expected outcome.

### Mission Alignment

Map recommended actions to mission priorities and expected government impact.

### Risk Watch

List top execution, governance, policy, or delivery risks with monitoring indicators.
