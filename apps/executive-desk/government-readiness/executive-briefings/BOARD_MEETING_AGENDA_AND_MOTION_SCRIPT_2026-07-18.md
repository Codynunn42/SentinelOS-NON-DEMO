### Evidence Response Script for Question 1

Question:
Does the evidence support management's scalability claim?

Recommended response:
Yes. The evidence supports the claim based on three facts captured in repository artifacts and execution telemetry.

1. Prior scaling friction patterns did not recur during a dense concurrent validation sprint across recovery resilience, tenant isolation, and endpoint execution.
2. The Evidence Vault and Executive Decision Register maintained traceable, synchronized action-to-receipt behavior under load, with no observed data drift in the reviewed run artifacts.
3. The Governance Engine preserved strict negative controls and correctly returned an Incomplete state when required controls were missing, demonstrating policy integrity under pressure rather than false-positive pass behavior.

Bottom line for the board:
The system preserved deterministic governance boundaries at high velocity. This is evidence-backed scalability, not a narrative claim.
