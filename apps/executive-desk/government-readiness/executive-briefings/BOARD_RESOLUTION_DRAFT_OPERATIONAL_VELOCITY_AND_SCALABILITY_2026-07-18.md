## Resolution

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors has reviewed the Chief Executive Directive dated July 18, 2026, regarding recent high-velocity operational validation actions and structural resilience findings across Sentinel OS and The Executive Desk;

WHEREAS, the Board acknowledges that the validation sprint was executed as a deliberate read-only stress exercise against the Government Deployment Blueprint (GBP) to evaluate governance integrity, evidence durability, and control-plane behavior under elevated operational pressure;

WHEREAS, the Board affirms that governance gates are structural trust boundaries and not cosmetic compliance markers, and that institutional mission authority requires deterministic enforcement, auditable evidence, and human-intent validation;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board hereby:

1. Accepts the CEO attestation and operational findings as presented.
2. Ratifies the governance principle that no critical gate may be bypassed for superficial compliance.
3. Confirms that scale authorization must remain evidence-conditioned and subject to executive approval controls.
4. Acknowledges management's conclusion that the Governance Engine, Evidence Vault, and XE Control Surface demonstrated structural resilience under stress conditions.
5. Directs management to transition from stress-validation velocity to calibrated operational predictability for all external engagements, including CISA and DOE pathways.
6. Reaffirms that all strategic initiatives must follow the proof-before-scale lifecycle:

Problem -> Evidence -> Workflow -> Pilot -> Measurement -> Executive Review -> Approval -> Scale

1. Requires quarterly reporting to the Board on:

- Governance gate integrity
- Evidence retrieval and audit readiness
- Model concentration and portability risk
- Infrastructure accountability completion
- Pilot-to-scale decision quality

1. Authorizes management to proceed with phased rollout under the documented strategic and federal readiness plans, subject to continued gate compliance and risk controls.
