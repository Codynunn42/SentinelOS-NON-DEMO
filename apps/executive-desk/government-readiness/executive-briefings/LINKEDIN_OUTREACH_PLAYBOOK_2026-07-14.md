# LinkedIn Outreach Playbook

**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Approach:** Relationships first.

## Daily Target

- 5-10 quality connection requests.
- One thoughtful follow-up to accepted connections.
- No product pitch.

## Connection Request Pattern

Use a short, respectful message tied to shared mission interests.

Template:
Hello [Name], I appreciate your work in [agency/mission area]. I am focused on helping leadership teams strengthen decision readiness and measurable mission outcomes. I would value connecting.

## Follow-Up Pattern (After Acceptance)

Template:
Thank you for connecting. I am currently building executive-focused materials around governed modernization, evidence-backed operations, and mission outcomes in government contexts. If useful, I would appreciate learning what modernization challenges are most pressing in your lane.

## Quality Filter

Send requests only when at least one condition is true:

- direct mission relevance,
- clear DOE or federal alignment,
- decision or modernization leadership role,
- strong shared network/context.

- Stay concise.
- Stay outcome-focused.
- Do not send links in first touch unless requested.
- Prioritize credibility and continuity over volume.
