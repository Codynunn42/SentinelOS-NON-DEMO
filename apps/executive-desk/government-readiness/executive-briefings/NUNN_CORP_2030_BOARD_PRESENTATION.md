## Slide 2 - Why Now

### Market Shift Is Structural

From:

- Who has the best AI model?

To:

- Who can govern AI responsibly and prove outcomes?

What changed:

- Regulation and policy scrutiny increased.
- Procurement is favoring auditability and accountability.
- Executive teams need proof before scale decisions.
- Model capabilities are commoditizing faster than governance trust.

Board takeaway:
Governance capability is becoming a core competitive advantage.

---

## Slide 3 - Strategic Thesis

### Define and Lead Executive AI Governance

Nunn Corporation strategic thesis:

- The highest-value AI opportunity is operating AI with executive authority, policy controls, and evidence-grade accountability.

Category statement:

- Nunn Corporation builds Executive Operating Systems that govern AI, infrastructure, and critical workflows with evidence-based decision making.

Board decision ask:

- Approve category-led positioning as enterprise strategy.

## Slide 4 - Doctrine

### Mission and Non-Negotiables

Mission:

- Govern AI. Enable Decisions. Preserve Human Authority.

Non-negotiables:

- No autonomous production action without human authority.
- No scale without measurable proof and executive sign-off.
- No workflow without policy mapping and evidence retention.
- No mission-critical single-model concentration without contingency.
- No major deployment without infrastructure accountability review.

Board takeaway:
Doctrine is a governance control system, not marketing language.

## Slide 6 - Proof-Before-Scale Standard

### Mandatory Lifecycle for Every Program

Problem -> Evidence -> Workflow -> Pilot -> Measurement -> Executive Review -> Approval -> Scale

What this enforces:

- Repeatable risk discipline
- Transparent value validation
- Better capital allocation decisions
- Fewer uncontrolled production commitments

Board decision ask:

- Ratify this lifecycle as mandatory operating policy.

## Slide 7 - Differentiator: Model Independence

### Stable Governance, Flexible Model Selection

Operating principle:

- The Executive Desk selects the appropriate model while policy, evidence, security, and authority remain constant.

Strategic value:

- Reduces provider concentration risk
- Preserves leverage in procurement
- Protects roadmap from model-cycle volatility
- Supports mission continuity during model transitions

Board KPI:
