## Position

Operational Upgrade is the SentinelOS framing for improving an existing operational motion without forcing the operator into a different system.

It is not legal recovery, dispute handling, autonomous contract repair, or a separate public product. It is a governed upgrade layer that makes obligations, evidence, approval requirements, drift, and receipts visible around the work an operator already performs.
