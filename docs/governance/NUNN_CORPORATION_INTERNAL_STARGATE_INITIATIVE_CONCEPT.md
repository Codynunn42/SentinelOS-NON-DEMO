# Nunn Corporation Internal Stargate Initiative — Working Concept

**Status:** Internal discussion draft; not approved for execution or external representation
**Owner:** Nunn Corporation
**Scope:** Proposed read-only, human-authorized workload and energy evaluation

## Naming and relationship boundary

Use **Nunn Corporation Internal Stargate Initiative** on first reference and
**Nunn Internal Stargate Initiative** as a short form. This is a working name
for Nunn Corporation's own proposed effort. It is separate from OpenAI's
Stargate AI infrastructure platform. No affiliation, partnership, endorsement,
shared ownership, or other relationship is implied. This distinction is for
clarity, not a challenge to OpenAI or its use of its name.

External materials must not imply that the two efforts are connected. Describe
any future partner, funding, or technical relationship only when supported by
specific, verifiable evidence and authorized for disclosure.

## Proposed technical evaluation

Evaluate, without controlling live operations, whether workload scheduling
around a defined operating window can support energy and grid objectives. A
future protocol could compare one baseline window with one alternative using
the same modeled constraints and appropriately sourced inputs. It should report:

- workload shift windows and the assumptions that define them;
- modeled energy consumed and peak-demand effects;
- operational, service, and other applicable constraints;
- uncertainty, missing inputs, and sensitivity to assumptions.

The study is read-only and requires human authorization. It must not issue
water-control, dispatch, or production commands. Modeled savings and modeled
effects must be labeled as estimates and kept separate from measured hardware
results and observed operational outcomes. No savings, capacity delivery, or
operational benefit is established by this concept.

## Evidence categories

Keep these categories distinct in proposals, reports, and executive updates:

| Category | What it can establish |
| --- | --- |
| Design commitments | A documented intended scope or design; not implementation or delivery |
| Financing agreements or awards | The specific funding or in-kind support documented by the agreement |
| Actual capacity delivery | Capacity made available and evidenced for the defined period and environment |
| Modeled results | Estimates from stated inputs, assumptions, and constraints |
| Measured hardware results | Results measured on identified hardware under a documented workload |
| Operational results | Outcomes observed in an authorized operational setting |

Do not treat a design commitment as financing, financing as capacity delivered,
or modeled savings as measured hardware or operational results.
