SentinelOS is a multi-tenant execution system that deploys business operations as isolated surface planes.

SentinelOS:
- executes workflows
- persists and audits actions
- scales across clients without rebuilding

## Operating Language
OwnerFi has two governed uses that must not be confused:

- OwnerFi remains the first active proof surface for the governed deal-execution
  reference implementation.
- OwnerFi is also the internal financial management domain for Nunn
  Corporation. AI Financial Management belongs inside OwnerFi as a capability
  set, not as a parallel effort.

HotelOps is the next placeholder surface, proving the core can expand by adding tenant-specific planes instead of cloning the platform.

Deal Execution Engine generates customer revenue. SINTENEX / Sintinex governs
commercial packaging and launch routing. OwnerFi records, analyzes, forecasts,
approves, and reports internal financial operations. SentinelOS orchestrates the
governance and receipt layer across those domains.
