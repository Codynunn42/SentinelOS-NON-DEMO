## Board-Safe Narrative

Sentinel is the Authority Layer for Artificial Intelligence.

Organizations are adopting AI faster than they can govern it. Sentinel ensures
that every AI action has a verified origin, an approved authority chain, and a
permanent record of accountability.

Before AI acts, Sentinel verifies intent.

Before AI results become institutional truth, Sentinel verifies outcomes.

The result is a continuous lineage of authority that transforms AI from an
opaque automation tool into a governed institutional asset.

Sentinel does not replace people. Sentinel ensures people remain accountable
for what AI does.
