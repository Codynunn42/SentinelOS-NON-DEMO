## Purpose

Record the SentinelOS shift from a document-centric workflow to an operational
governance workflow.

In this model, documents are not the workflow itself. Documents are evidence
generated by a repeatable operating process: intake, classification, review,
recommendation, owner decision, next gate, and cadence.

## Operating Shift

```yaml
operating_shift:
  from: document_centric_workflow
  to: operational_governance_workflow
  meaning: >
    SentinelOS uses repeatable review jobs and department queues to generate
    evidence, recommendations, and next gates. Documentation records the work;
    it does not replace the work.
```

## Department Function

```yaml
VR_Department:
  official_name: Virtual Review Department
  organizational_function: permanent_review_and_recommendation_function
  operating_role: >
    Receive work from Mission Control or Executive Desk, create VR Jobs,
    coordinate review queues, classify evidence, produce recommendations, and
    return next gates for owner decision.

    - deploy

    - activate_billing
    - activate_checkout
    - publish_public_proof
    - approve_customer_production
```

## VR Job Lifecycle

```yaml
vr_job_lifecycle:
  1_request:
    description: Work enters from Executive Desk, Mission Control, SINTENEX, cadence, or owner direction.
  2_job_created:
    description: A VR Job receives an ID, title, source gate, evidence inputs, and review groups.
  3_classification:
    description: Work is classified by route, authority, public/protected/internal/held posture, and evidence state.
  4_department_reviews:
    description: Architecture, cybersecurity, governance, platform, runtime, documentation, compliance, infrastructure, or optimization queues review the job.
  5_recommendations:
    description: The VR Department records findings, risks, recommended order, and next gates.
  6_executive_desk_review:
    description: Executive Desk presents the review in guided, owner-accountable language.
  7_owner_decision:
    description: Owner approves, reschedules, declines, or holds the next gate.
  8_cadence:
    description: The decision returns to cadence with the next required gate and holds preserved.
  authority_created: false
```

## Department Queues

```yaml
department_queues:
  security_queue:
    purpose: Review authentication, authorization, route hardening, secrets, dependencies, and least privilege.
  architecture_queue:
    purpose: Review repository layout, API boundaries, integration shape, and scalability.
  runtime_queue:
    purpose: Review Mission Control, route health, runtime-upgrade candidates, receipts, and audit surfaces.
  governance_queue:
    purpose: Review policy compliance, approval boundaries, authority chain, and non-authorization controls.
  documentation_queue:
    purpose: Review consistency, clarity, evidence quality, unsupported claims, and cadence alignment.
  compliance_queue:
    purpose: Review certification, regulatory, contractual, and external-claim boundaries without creating compliance claims.
  infrastructure_queue:
    purpose: Review cloud, database, network, deployment, and platform integration posture without mutating runtime.
  optimization_queue:
    purpose: Review simplification, performance, usability, and reliability recommendations.
  authority_created: false
```

## Institutional Rule

Every significant engineering change, governance update, infrastructure
modification, security assessment, public-proof proposal, runtime-upgrade
proposal, or customer-production proposal should enter the VR Department as a
VR Job when multidisciplinary review is required.

The VR Department may recommend. It may not execute.
