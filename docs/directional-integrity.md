# Directional Integrity

**COMM:** Sentinel AI by Cody Nunn | Nunn Cloud

Directional integrity is the discipline of keeping operational movement aligned with the system's north star as context, pressure, and opportunity change.

SentinelOS uses directional integrity to distinguish healthy progression from drift.

## Core Question

```txt
Does this movement preserve the verified direction of the system?
```

## Drift Types

SentinelOS evaluates movement through simple drift categories:

- `straight`: aligned with current verified direction
- `bend`: a change in angle that may still preserve intent
- `fork`: a split into competing direction
- `drift`: gradual misalignment from the north star
- `break`: movement that violates the current authority or legitimacy model

## Use

Directional integrity helps decide whether to:

- continue
- reconcile
- revise
- hold
- request a new planning packet

It does not authorize execution. It informs governance review and controlled outcome selection.
