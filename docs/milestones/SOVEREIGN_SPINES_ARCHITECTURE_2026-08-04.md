# Sovereign Spines Architecture

## SentinelOS Phase 3 Blueprint

The Sentinel Bridge has evolved into three sovereign execution spines that operate as the backbone of the deployed runtime:

- SIS — Sovereign Identity Spine
- SRS — Sovereign Routing Spine
- SGS — Sovereign Governance Spine

These spines are the natural evolution of the sovereign runtime now that Phase 2 evidence completion has been validated.

## 1. SIS — Sovereign Identity Spine

### Purpose

Bind identity to session, evidence, and governance.

### Inputs

- passportId
- institutionId
- governed session
- capability envelope
- module mapping

### Outputs

- identity-linked evidence
- identity-governance-denial
- identity completeness signals
- identity routing constraints

### Why it matters

Identity becomes a first-class sovereign object. Every evidence chain becomes identity-aware.

## 2. SRS — Sovereign Routing Spine

### Purpose

Govern routing across providers, models, modules, and classification.

### Inputs

- capabilityId
- moduleId
- providerId
- modelId
- dataClassification
- identity constraints

### Outputs

- routing evidence
- routing-governance-denial
- routing optimization signals
- routing completeness

### Why it matters

Routing becomes governed, predictable, and evidence-driven.

## 3. SGS — Sovereign Governance Spine

### Purpose

Enforce governance across modules, identities, providers, and routing.

### Inputs

- module boundaries
- identity boundaries
- provider boundaries
- routing boundaries
- evidence completeness

### Outputs

- governance-denial evidence
- governance reasoning
- governance prediction
- governance risk scoring

### Why it matters

Governance becomes dynamic, evidence-aware, and enforceable at runtime.

## 4. C7 Integration Map

| Spine | Activated During | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIS | Identity onboarding | Bind identity to evidence chain |
| SRS | Workflow routing activation | Govern routing decisions |
| SGS | Governance enforcement | Enforce module, identity, and provider boundaries |

This is the sovereign backbone of the deployed runtime.

## 5. Phase 3 Evolution Map

| Spine | Autonomous Form | New capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIS | A-SIS | Identity-risk scoring, identity-governance reasoning |
| SRS | A-SRS | Routing optimization, evidence-driven routing |
| SGS | A-SGS | Governance prediction, governance risk scoring |

Phase 3 is where SentinelOS becomes autonomous rather than merely governed.

## 6. Institutional Launch Packet Mapping

- Identity Manifest -> SIS
- Routing Manifest -> SRS
- Governance Manifest -> SGS
- Evidence-State Summary -> all three spines
- ORV-4 Deployment Score -> validates all spines
- Readiness Declaration -> final C7 output

This packet becomes the institution's sovereign deployment certificate.

## 7. Strategic Outcome

The three spines convert the validated runtime into a full sovereign operating system for institutional deployment. They provide the architectural foundation for C7 deployment and the autonomous evolution planned for Phase 3.
