## Mission Sentence

```txt
SentinelOS helps business surface planes operate with governed execution, approval discipline, audit visibility, and evidence-before-claims control.
```

## Executive Overview

```txt
OwnerFi is the first active business surface plane. SentinelOS is the governed system layer underneath it: the layer that controls what workflows are allowed to do, when approval is required, how execution remains bounded, and how evidence stays visible before claims are made.

Operational Upgrade is the modernization lane for existing relationships and workflows. Contract Reclamation is a sibling governed faceplane lane for contract-state reconstruction and evidence intelligence. Both remain domain surfaces that inherit governance boundaries; neither becomes the SentinelOS core.
```

## Operating Philosophy

```txt
Governance before expansion. Evidence before claims. Business outcomes before technical explanation. Surface planes may produce review artifacts, but authority remains bounded by SentinelOS governance controls and separate approvals.
```

## Governance Philosophy

```txt
Governance is pre-execution control, not post-execution logging. Audit trails, receipts, metrics, snapshots, and review artifacts improve visibility, but they do not grant permission, authorize publication, activate pilots, or create runtime authority.
```

## Internal Narrative Sequence

Use this order for internal meeting preparation:

1. Start with the business surface: OwnerFi.
2. Explain the business outcome before the technical mechanism.
3. State that SentinelOS governs the operating layer beneath the surface.
4. Show that governance blocks or requires approval before execution.
5. Use audit and receipts as evidence, not as authority.
6. Position Operational Upgrade as modernization and continuity.
7. Position Contract Reclamation as sibling contract-state reconstruction.
8. Close with non-claims: no billing, funnels, publication, legal advice, recovery claim, pilot activation, deployment, or runtime mutation unless separately approved and verified.
