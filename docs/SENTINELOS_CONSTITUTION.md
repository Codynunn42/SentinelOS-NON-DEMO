# SentinelOS Constitution

**COMM:** Sentinel AI by Cody Nunn | Nunn Cloud
**Adopted:** C5.0
**Status:** `[RATIFIED]`

---

*SentinelOS — Sovereign Institutional Operating Environment*

---

## Preamble

SentinelOS is a sovereign operating environment designed to unify institutional capability, governance,
evidence, and executive oversight into a single coherent platform. It abstracts complexity, enforces
integrity, and ensures that every action taken within the institution is governed, observable, and
accountable.

This Constitution defines the principles, guarantees, and architectural commitments that govern
SentinelOS. All future expansions, modules, providers, and capabilities must adhere to these
principles.

---

## I. Principle of Institutional Simplicity

SentinelOS shall present **one coherent operating environment** regardless of the number or diversity
of underlying providers.

External systems are adopted as capability providers and exposed through **Institutional Modules** that
operate under common governance, evidence, security, and executive oversight.

> The institution experiences simplicity.
> SentinelOS manages complexity.

---

## II. Principle of Provider Abstraction

Providers are **implementation details**, not institutional concepts.

The institution interacts with:

- Modules
- Capabilities
- Governance
- Evidence
- Executive Desk

It does **not** interact with:

- Provider names
- Provider identities
- Provider infrastructure

Providers may change, scale, fail, or be replaced without altering the institutional experience.

---

## III. Principle of Governed Capability

Every capability in SentinelOS — regardless of provider — is governed by:

- Sovereign Policy Engine (SPE)
- Sovereign Evidence Ledger (SEL)
- Sovereign Executive Review (SER)

No capability may execute without:

1. Policy validation
2. Evidence generation
3. Executive visibility

Governance is not optional.
Governance is the operating model.

---

## IV. Principle of Evidence Integrity

Every action taken within SentinelOS must produce:

- A governed session
- A receipt
- A ledger entry
- A cross-provider evidence chain

Evidence must be:

- Immutable
- Verifiable
- Executively visible
- Provider-agnostic

Evidence is the backbone of institutional trust.

---

## V. Principle of Modular Coherence

Institutional Modules are the primary abstraction SentinelOS exposes.

A Module:

- Owns a purpose
- Aggregates capabilities
- Aggregates provider health
- Inherits governance
- Presents a unified institutional surface

Modules must make SentinelOS feel like **one operating environment**, not a collection of systems.

Any expansion must pass the **Coherence Test**:

> "Does this make SentinelOS feel more unified, or more fragmented?"

If fragmented → rejected.

---

## VI. Principle of AI Governance

AI is an institutional capability — not a special case.

AI Operations must:

- Use the Docking Protocol
- Use the Capability Broker
- Produce evidence
- Follow policy
- Respect data classification
- Route through the Model Broker

AI must be governed identically to all other institutional capabilities.

There are no exceptions.

---

## VII. Principle of Sovereign Runtime Stability

The sovereign runtime — SPE, SEL, SER, SNCS — is the constitutional core of SentinelOS.

It must remain:

- Stable
- Predictable
- Backwards-compatible
- Provider-agnostic
- Module-agnostic

No module, provider, or expansion may alter the sovereign runtime.

The runtime is the Constitution's enforcement mechanism.

---

## VIII. Principle of Executive Oversight

The Executive Desk is the authoritative institutional surface.

It must provide:

- Module health
- Governance state
- Evidence summaries
- Drift detection
- Compliance visibility
- Operational readiness

Executives must be able to understand the institution's operational state **without technical
knowledge**.

---

## IX. Principle of Operational Readiness

Every major expansion must pass Operational Readiness Validation (ORV).

- ORV-1 validates the sovereign runtime.
- ORV-2 validates the module layer.
- ORV-N validates future institutional tiers.

No expansion may proceed without:

- Confidence ≥ 90/100
- Evidence completeness = 1.0
- All failure scenarios handled

Operational readiness is the gatekeeper of institutional safety.

---

## X. Principle of Institutional Continuity

SentinelOS must ensure continuity across:

- Provider changes
- Module expansions
- Capability additions
- Policy updates
- Infrastructure evolution

Continuity means:

- No regressions
- No governance gaps
- No evidence gaps
- No executive blind spots

Continuity is the guarantee of institutional stability.

---

## XI. Principle of Sovereign Expansion

SentinelOS must support expansion into:

- Government systems
- Agency systems
- Sovereign capability tiers
- Regulated environments
- Classified environments

Expansion must preserve:

- Architectural consistency
- Governance integrity
- Evidence continuity
- Executive visibility
- Institutional coherence

Expansion must never compromise sovereignty.

---

## XII. Principle of Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution supersedes:

- Provider preferences
- Module preferences
- Implementation shortcuts
- Convenience decisions
- External system constraints

If a design decision conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution wins.

Always.

---

## Ratification

This Constitution is adopted as the governing document of SentinelOS and shall be referenced for all
future architectural, governance, and institutional decisions.
