## Strategic Position

The strongest first lane remains the **Office of Nuclear Energy (NE)**. NE’s mission focus on nuclear energy science and technology aligns with governed AI support for scientific workflows, decision support, maintenance analysis, and evidence-heavy operations.

All engagement remains bounded by these controls:

- No unsupported claims of operational control.
- No unsupported claims of safety authority.
- Human authority, traceability, and evidence integrity remain primary.

## Lane Guidance

### 1) Office of Nuclear Energy — primary lane

This is the concentration point for near-term relationship-building. Current NE relationship context (including Lauren Joyce) is directly relevant to mission-fit discovery.

The message posture is:

> **“We are developing governed AI capability for high-trust scientific workflows. We have now submitted a DOE Genesis autonomous-laboratories concept. Where could this be genuinely useful within the nuclear mission, and who should we learn from?”**

### 2) National laboratories — strongest technical lane

Use targeted alignment, not broad outreach. Build a shortlist based on published overlap with:

- nuclear-energy / reactor research,
- autonomous experimentation,
- AI for science,
- nuclear-security scientific workflows.

### 3) M&O contractors / primes — acquisition pathway lane

Commercial posture stays:

> **Governed-AI capability provider / specialist subcontractor / integration partner**

This preserves mission ownership with incumbents while enabling practical entry through existing procurement and site operating structures.

### 4) Environmental Management — adjacent, distinct mission lane

EM fit exists around planning/review, evidence-heavy workflows, anomaly triage, post-event reconstruction, and remote operations support.

Do not reuse the autonomous-lab pitch unchanged for EM; shape to EM mission realities first.

### 5) NNSA — high-value, highest-sensitivity lane

Approach with strict restraint and discovery-first framing.

Initial question:

> **Is there an unclassified, appropriately bounded scientific or engineering workflow where governed AI research would be relevant?**
