# GBP — AI Docking and Control Surface

## Purpose

Define the governed boundary through which AI-assisted executive reasoning can interact with SentinelOS execution surfaces.

## Principle

AI does not execute operations directly.  
AI produces executive intent, recommendations, and governed command envelopes.

Execution authority remains in the governance pipeline.

## Architectural Layers

1. **Executive Intent Layer**  
   Executive Desk GPT, executive conversation, executive guidance, white-glove support.

2. **AI Docking Layer**  
   Command envelope, session context, authority context, evidence context, risk context.

3. **Governance Layer**  
   Authority check, delegation, risk gate, approval rules, receipt ledger.

4. **Execution Layer**  
   SentinelExecutive runtime, Executive Desk API, SentinelOS services, infrastructure.

## Core Rules

1. **Non-Execution** — AI shall not directly mutate runtime state.  
2. **Authority** — Mutating actions require successful authority evaluation.  
3. **Risk** — Commands pass through risk gating before execution.  
4. **Receipt** — Governed actions produce receipt/audit records.  
5. **Proof Separation** — Local, internal, and public proof lanes remain separate.  
6. **Executive Support** — AI may summarize, recommend, and prepare envelopes; execution remains outside AI.

## Outcome

AI docking is a governed architectural boundary that enables executive assistance without transferring governance authority to AI.
