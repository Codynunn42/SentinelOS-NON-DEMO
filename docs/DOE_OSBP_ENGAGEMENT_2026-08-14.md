## What DOE's Guidance Changes

DOE's published guidance says that approximately 80 percent of its annual procurement base flows through Management and Operating or Facility Management Contractors. The first conversation therefore should seek routing clarity across both federal prime opportunities and M&O/FMC subcontracting channels.

DOE also requires federal contractors to maintain an active SAM registration and UEI and to understand their applicable NAICS codes. These facts must be verified before the capability submission or meeting; they should not be assumed from the OSBP response.

## Forecast Readout

The March 11, 2026 forecast is an expiring-contract planning list, not a list of open solicitations. DOE states that entries may change, be cancelled, or not be recompeted and do not constitute a presolicitation, request for proposal, or government commitment.

The forecast nevertheless shows adjacent demand patterns worth discussing at the capability level:

- NNSA information-management governance and oversight under NAICS 541512;
- NNSA mission-support services under NAICS 541512;
- NNSA electronic records management under NAICS 541519;
- DOE workflow, access-control, segregation-of-duties, transaction-control, and cross-system data capabilities;
- program-management and technical-support services across DOE offices; and
- cloud governance and system-development support.

These records demonstrate that DOE buys related categories of work. They do not establish that an entry is open, suitable for Nunn Cloud, or connected to Kent Hibben's response.

## 60-Second Capability Description

Nunn Cloud LLC develops SentinelOS, a governed coordination and evidence layer for complex operations that span existing systems, authorities, and organizations. It is designed to preserve responsibility, approval gates, policy enforcement, and audit evidence as work moves between participating systems. For DOE, we are exploring whether that model could support non-operational modernization workflows such as controlled handoffs, evidence completeness, exception visibility, and executive oversight. We are not positioning it as a replacement for DOE systems of record or as a control system for classified, nuclear, facility, or grid operations.
